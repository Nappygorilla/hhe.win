About
Luna.

A minimal storefront and account experience built around Luna's gaming software catalog.

Luna.win is designed to keep product information, access, updates, service status, and support in one clean place. The visual system is intentionally monochrome: black, white, and moonlight.

What this site does

The public site is the storefront layer. Product pages explain what is available, the account area is the home for customer access, and the status page communicates service state.

What comes next

Authentication, payments, licensing, and real-time status can be connected to the frontend when the backend is ready.
