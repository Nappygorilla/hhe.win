FAQ / SUPPORT

Questions,
answered.

Common questions about products, access, accounts, and the Luna storefront.

Is Roblox External free?
Yes. Roblox External is the free product. A lifetime key is a separate $5 option.
What does the $5 lifetime key mean?
The lifetime key is the paid lifetime-access option for the Roblox External product.
Which products are currently available?
Roblox External is currently the live product. CS2, Fortnite, Call of Duty, FiveM, Temp Spoofer, Perm Spoofer, and Gorilla Tag are shown as upcoming categories.
Where do I find updates?
Use the Updates page for storefront and product changes, and the Status page for service incidents or maintenance.
Can I change my display name?
The account interface includes a display-name surface. Persistent changes require the account backend to be connected.
Where can I get support?
Use the Contact page for support requests and the Status page first when checking for a known service issue.
