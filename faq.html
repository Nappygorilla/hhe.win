FAQ / 01

Questions,
answered.

Common storefront and account questions. Product-specific details should be added as your release information is finalized.

01 / PRODUCT

What is Luna Roblox External?

It is the current Roblox product category on the Luna storefront. The dedicated product page is the source for its public release information.

02 / ACCESS

Where is my purchase?

Purchased access is intended to appear in the account dashboard once the backend is connected.

03 / UPDATES

Where are updates posted?

Use the product page for release information and the status page for service incidents or maintenance.

04 / SUPPORT

How do I contact support?

Use the support page and include the relevant account or storefront information needed to identify the issue.

05 / AVAILABILITY

Which products are live?

Roblox External is currently marked available. The other catalog categories are presented as coming soon.

06 / ACCOUNT

Can I change my display name?

The dashboard is structured to support account settings, including a display-name control when account integration is added.
